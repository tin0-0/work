Who We Are

SPARE PARTS SPECIALISTS

At BK Auto Spares, we are committed to keeping vehicles running at their best with high-quality, reliable spare parts. Based in the UAE, we specialize in sourcing and supplying genuine and aftermarket auto components for a wide range of makes and models.

Whether you're a workshop, distributor, or car owner, our team ensures fast delivery, competitive pricing, and expert support tailored to your needs. Zimbabwe remains our primary export destination and we're proud to bridge the gap between Dubai's parts market and Africa's automotive needs.
